# Story themes from real enquiries (anonymised)

These situations come from patterns across about 1,550 real founder enquiries (CRM, Nov 2026).

**Confidentiality rule, always:**
- Never use a real name, company, brand, product name, town, phone, amount or date from the CRM.
- Use the situation only.
- Give every script invented names and changed details: different sector, city and numbers.
- Combine two situations when it helps.
- Never write anything that could let a real person recognise themselves.
- Never add CRM text to this repo.

Why these work: they are what our audience is actually living through. Use each as the *conflict* of a reel. Open on the painful moment in the first second, then the fix (our topic), then the keyword.

## Who is enquiring (shares across the CRM)

- Most enquiries are founders asking for **investment / funding**: about half mention equity, shares or investors.
- About 1 in 10 also ask for **mentorship / guidance**.
- **About 1 in 5 messages from abroad** (Gulf numbers: UAE, Qatar, Oman, Bahrain, Saudi). Many are NRIs planning to come back and start.
- **Idea / concept stage is very common.** Often there is no company, no registration and no pitch deck yet.
- Small existing businesses (shops, food, farms, trading, manufacturing) want money to **expand**.
- **Busy, call back later, will discuss with partner** is the most common reply. People stall without a nudge.

## Situations (use as reel conflicts)

### Company registration / structure (co)
1. Asked investors for money while the business was **not registered at all**. The first question was "which company?"
2. Runs it as a **proprietorship or trust**. The investor can't get shares in that.
3. Two friends started it on **trust, nothing written**. The "who owns what" question comes up only when money comes in.
4. **NRI in the Gulf** wants to start in Kerala before moving back and assumes he can't be a director.
5. Was running two businesses under one name. A bank or investor asked to separate them.
6. Registered in another state long ago, now operating in Kerala. Grants and local schemes ask for **Kerala registration**.

### GST (gst)
7. A **women's clothing brand** selling on Instagram wants to export, so she needs GST plus an import-export code.
8. A **food / home-products** seller gets a big shop or hotel order, and the buyer says "send a GST bill".
9. A service business's (agency, IT, consultant) **corporate client** won't pay without a GSTIN on the invoice.
10. Turnover has quietly crossed the limit while the owner was "too busy to look".
11. Wants to sell on marketplaces to **other states** and is told GST is needed first.

### Investor agreements (inv)
12. **Pitch rejected** for "no market validation, no sales data". Lesson: traction before ask. Fix papers and numbers first.
13. **Friends-and-family money** came in with no agreement. Now one wants it back, with interest, at the worst time.
14. Planned to raise from **many small investors** (a "growth partner" plan: 20+ people with small cheques). Risky: private placement allows at most 200 people a year, and a pooled-returns scheme can fall under strict SEBI rules. Needs a proper structure.
15. Co-founders' **equity split agreed on WhatsApp** (e.g. 70/30). There's no vesting, so one leaves after 6 months still holding 30%.
16. **Investor (Gulf Malayali)** wants to put money into a cousin's Kerala startup. He asks what protects him: SHA, board seat, exit clause, FEMA reporting.
17. Took a **bank loan instead** of equity. It's fine, but a founder should know the difference: loan = repay, equity = give up a share.
18. Investor said yes, money arrived, and the founder **spent it before the shares were allotted** and filed. That's a compliance problem.
19. Founder keeps saying "**I'll talk to my partner and call back**". The deal dies in the delay; a term sheet with dates keeps it moving.
20. An investor asked for a **pitch deck and data room**. The founder had only the idea in his head.

### Startup India / DPIIT (sui)
21. Started as an **egg farm, switched to mushrooms** with a new process. Thinks "startup" means only apps, but innovative traditional businesses can be recognised too.
22. Applied for a **government grant** and was rejected for "no DPIIT recognition".
23. A **proprietorship** applies for DPIIT and is refused. They must register a company / LLP first.
24. Thinks DPIIT recognition = automatic tax holiday. It's a **separate application**.
25. A **student / young founder** with a prototype (education app, sports app for kids, robotics) wants credibility before meeting investors.

### Kerala Startup Mission (sk)
26. A **Gulf returnee** with 10+ years of software experience is coming back to Kerala to build his product and doesn't know KSUM exists.
27. Has the idea and a prototype, and wants the **idea grant / incubation**, but has no Unique ID.
28. Kerala company that **never registered with KSUM** and missed a grant call.
29. **Woman founder** (health food, activewear, ayurveda) wants mentoring and an incubation space in Kochi or Trivandrum.
30. A college founder with a **campus incubator** prototype asks what comes after the demo day.

## Hook patterns that fit these (first 1 second, max 38 characters a line)

- Quote the painful line: "ഇൻവെസ്റ്റർ ചോദിച്ചു: കമ്പനി ഏതാ?"
- Number-loss: "30% ഷെയർ, 6 മാസം കഴിഞ്ഞ് അവൻ പോയി"
- Rejection: "പിച്ച് റിജക്റ്റ്. കാരണം ഒറ്റ വാക്ക്"
- Gulf: "ദുബായിൽ ഇരുന്ന് നാട്ടിൽ കമ്പനി?"
- Myth flip: "DPIIT കിട്ടിയാൽ ടാക്സ് ഇല്ല? തെറ്റ്"
